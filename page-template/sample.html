Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing

Why does my drain keep blocking again after it has been cleared?

A drain that blocks again soon after cleaning may still hold debris, or a pipe defect may be catching waste. We can use a CCTV camera to check accessible pipes for roots and damage. We then explain whether further cleaning or a repair is the better option.

Do you clear sewer and stormwater drains in Aspendale?

Yes. We clear blockages in sewer and stormwater pipework and choose equipment that suits the affected line. We check where water is backing up and look for the cause before we start. If cleaning shows damaged sections, we talk you through the repair options.

Can you help with an overflowing drain outside business hours?

We provide 24/7 emergency plumbing support for urgent problems such as serious blockages and overflowing toilets. When you call, we ask what is happening and confirm when we can attend. Understanding the situation first helps us advise you on the right next step.

How is the price for drain cleaning in Aspendale worked out?

We look at access, where the blockage sits and the equipment needed to clear it. A camera inspection or repair work may add to the job. We explain the proposed scope and pricing before we begin and discuss any extra findings along the way.

Can a damaged drain be fixed without replacing the whole pipe?

Often it can. We assess whether a localised repair or relining will address the damaged section, depending on the pipe condition, layout and extent of the damage. If replacement is needed, we explain which sections need attention and what access the work requires.
